## What it does

| Effect | Detail |
|:---|:---|
| **Powder snow walking** | The horse walks across powder snow instead of falling through |
| **Fall damage reduction** | Softens the damage from drops |

That combination makes hooves the default choice for mountains, snowy biomes, and anywhere the terrain is vertical. For *big* drops you want the [Stabilizer](horse-stabilizer) instead. Hooves reduce fall damage; the stabilizer can cancel it.

---

## Frost Walker

Hooves accept the **Frost Walker** enchantment, applied at an **anvil** from an enchanted book.

A horse wearing enchanted hooves leaves a trail of **frosted ice** as it runs over water, exactly like a player in frost-walker boots. Rivers, lakes, and coastline stop being obstacles. You just ride across.

### Built for speed

The freeze keeps up with a fast horse. At **15+ blocks per second** the freeze radius scales up with your speed, so you don't outrun the ice and drop through a gap in the trail.

{: .warning }
> Frost Walker freezes water. **Lava is still lava.** Riding into it will kill your horse regardless of what it's wearing.

---

## Disabling

Hooves can be switched off server-side with the `hooves` config toggle. Disabled, the item stays craftable in **dummy mode** as a decorative model but grants no effects. See [Configuration](../configuration).
