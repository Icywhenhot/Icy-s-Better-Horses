# Canister
{: .no_toc }

A small copper-and-gold capsule. It does nothing on its own, but the [Stabilizer](horse-stabilizer) can't be built without two of them.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

---

## Crafting

{% include craft.html id="canister" %}

You need **two canisters** per stabilizer, so budget 12 copper and 4 gold.

---

## What it's for

The canister is the working end of the stabilizer, the part that actually deploys and catches the fall. Craft two, then build the [Horse Stabilizer](horse-stabilizer) around them.

It has no use by itself and can't be equipped to a horse.

{: .note }
> The canister counts as a Better Horses item, so it works as the ingredient for crafting a spare [Stable Handbook](stable-handbook).
