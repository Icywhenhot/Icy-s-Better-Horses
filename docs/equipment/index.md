## The gear tree

Almost everything starts with the [Upgraded Saddle](upgraded-saddle). It's the only piece that fits a horse directly; the rest slot into it.

```
Upgraded Saddle ──┬── Chest / Ender Chest   (storage)
                  ├── Horseshoe             (snow walking, fall damage)
                  ├── Horse Medkit          (emergency heal)
                  ├── Carpet                (cosmetic, any colour)
                  └── Harness slot ──┬── Glidewing Harness  ← Canister ×2
                                     └── Horse Cart         ← Wheel ×2
```

{: .warning }
> The **Glidewing Harness and the Horse Cart share one slot**. A horse either wears a harness or tows a cart, never both. Pick based on the trip: harness for mountains and ravines, cart for hauling.

Two slots don't need the saddle at all: vanilla **horse armour**, and a **banner** that hangs from it. Horse armour also takes armour enchantments from books at an anvil. See [Upgraded Saddle](upgraded-saddle#banners).

## At a glance

| Item | Slot | What it does | Worth it when |
|:---|:---|:---|:---|
| [Upgraded Saddle](upgraded-saddle) | Worn | Unlocks all five gear slots | Immediately. Everything needs it. |
| [Chest / Ender Chest](upgraded-saddle#chest-storage) | Chest | 27 slots, up to 54 on the right breed, or your shared ender inventory | Any trip where you're carrying loot |
| [Horseshoe](horse-hooves) | Horseshoe | Powder snow walking, reduced fall damage, takes Frost Walker | Mountains, snow, crossing water |
| [Horse Medkit](horse-medkit) | Medkit | One-shot auto-heal below half health | Nether trips, combat, anything risky |
| [Glidewing Harness](horse-stabilizer) | Harness | Slows long falls and can cancel the damage entirely | Cliffs, ravines, mountain routes |
| [Horse Cart](horse-cart) | Harness | Tows a 4-seat cart, optional double chest and plough | Bulk hauling, co-op travel, ploughing fields |
| [Carpet](upgraded-saddle#carpets) | Carpet | A per-breed blanket drawn in place of the saddle | Whenever you want your horse to look good |
| [Canister](canister) | Component | Component for the stabilizer | Only as a stabilizer ingredient |
| [Wheel](wheel) | Component | Component for the cart | Only as a cart ingredient |
| [Stable Handbook](stable-handbook) | Held | The in-game guide | Early, and keep a spare |

## Fitting gear quickly

Two shortcuts save a trip through the inventory screen:

- **Right-click the horse** holding a [Glidewing Harness](horse-stabilizer) or a [Cart](horse-cart) and it goes straight on, as long as the horse wears an Upgraded Saddle and that slot is empty.
- **Right-click a cart** with a Chest in hand to mount the chest in its bed, or with a hoe to fit a plough.

## Taking gear off

Things come off in the reverse order they went on, and the slot **flashes red** if you try to skip a step:

1. Shear the chest off the cart
2. Unhitch the cart
3. Then the saddle can come off

Chests must be emptied before removal, because a loaded chest refuses to come off rather than spilling its contents across the road.

## Config toggles

Three gear pieces can be switched off entirely by a server admin: harnesses, medkits, and horseshoes.

Disabled items don't vanish from the game and stay craftable. Gear already on a horse stays put and still shows, it just stops working, and new pieces can't be fitted. See [Configuration](../configuration#turning-gear-off).
