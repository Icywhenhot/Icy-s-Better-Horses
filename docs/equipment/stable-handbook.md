## Crafting

{% include craft.html id="stable_handbook" %}

A **shapeless** recipe: one **Book** plus **any Better Horses item**.

Any of these work as the second ingredient:

- [Upgraded Saddle](upgraded-saddle)
- Chest *(vanilla)*
- [Horse Hooves](horse-hooves)
- [Horse Medkit](horse-medkit)
- [Canister](canister)
- [Horse Stabilizer](horse-stabilizer)
- [Hitchpost](hitchpost)
- [Wheel](wheel)
- [Horse Cart](horse-cart)

Since a plain vanilla **Chest** counts, you can make a handbook before crafting anything else in the mod. A book and a chest is a first-day recipe.

{: .tip }
> Crafting one earns the **Stable Reading** advancement.

---

## What's inside

The handbook is powered by [Modonomicon](https://modrinth.com/mod/modonomicon) and organised into three categories:

| Category | Covers |
|:---|:---|
| **Barn Door Basics** | Claiming and bonding, the info screen, owner-only riding, breeding and traits, riding improvements, config |
| **Tack Room** | Every craft in the mod, with a note on when each piece is worth making |
| **Breeds** | A field guide to all fifteen breeds: appearance, coats, and where each lives |

Recipes render as real crafting grids inside the book, so you don't need to memorise layouts.

---

## Spares

It's cheap to remake, so keep one in your travel chest and one at home. Handing a spare to another player is the fastest way to explain the mod to them.

{: .note }
> Modonomicon is a **required dependency** of Better Horses, so the handbook is always available. You'll never have a world where the mod loads but the book recipe doesn't.
