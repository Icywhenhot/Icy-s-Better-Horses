## What it unlocks

Equip it on a horse and the horse's inventory screen gains **five gear slots**:

| Slot | Accepts |
|:---|:---|
| Chest | Chest or Ender Chest |
| Hooves | [Horse Hooves](horse-hooves) |
| Medkit | [Horse Medkit](horse-medkit) |
| Stabilizer | [Horse Stabilizer](horse-stabilizer) **or** [Horse Cart](horse-cart) |

---

## Chest storage

Drop a plain **Chest** into the chest slot for **27 storage slots** on the horse screen, a full single chest that walks around with you.

How many rows you get depends on the horse:

| Horse | Rows | Slots |
|:---|:---:|:---:|
| Most breeds | 3 | 27 |
| Any [draft](../breeds/index) horse | 4 | 36 |
| [Morgan](../breeds/morgan) at bond 100 | 4 | 36 |
| [Haflinger](../breeds/haflinger) | 4, **6** at bond 100 | 36, then 54 |
| [Belgian](../breeds/belgian) | **6** | 54 |

{: .warning }
> Empty the chest before removing it, or the contents drop on the ground.

### Ender Chest option

Use an **Ender Chest** instead and you get your **Ender Inventory**, the same one shared by every ender chest you own. That is always 27 slots, so a Belgian gains nothing from an ender chest over a normal one except reach.

That means a horse that can reach your base storage from the far side of the world. Swapping back to a normal chest, or pulling the ender chest out entirely, doesn't lose anything: ender inventory contents live with your player, not the horse.

{: .tip }
> Fitting an ender chest earns the **Pocket Dimension, Four Legs** advancement.

## The stats overlay

Hold the Upgraded Saddle and look at **any** horse, including wild, untamed ones, to show a **Horse Stats** panel above your hotbar:

- Gender
- Breed
- Speed in blocks per second
- Jump height in blocks

This is the quickest way to evaluate wild stock before committing to taming. See [Horse Info screen](../horse-info-screen#the-quick-stats-overlay).

---

## Removing it

The saddle comes off with **shears**, but only after everything else is off first. A horse towing a cart won't give up its saddle:

> Unhitch the cart before taking the saddle off.

Only the owner can shear a saddle off an owned horse.
