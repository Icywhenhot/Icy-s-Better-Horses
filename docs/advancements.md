## Notes on the harder ones

### Built Different (25 blocks/second)

This needs breeding *and* bonding. The mod's speed ceiling is 25.5 blk/s, so you need a horse with a strong base speed pushed the rest of the way by a full **+75%** bond bonus.

Route: breed a fast line following the [breeding loop](genetics#a-practical-breeding-loop) until stats stop improving, then bond the best foal to 100. Check progress on the [Horse Info screen](horse-info-screen), which displays speed in blk/s directly.

### Gotta Tame 'Em All (fifteen breeds)

The long one. Work from the [biome lookup table](breeds/#which-breed-lives-where) rather than wandering. A few breeds are effectively single-biome finds and worth targeting deliberately:

| Breed | Easiest biome |
|:---|:---|
| [Andalusian](breeds/andalusian) | Cherry Grove |
| [Icelandic](breeds/icelandic) | Ice Spikes |
| [Arabian](breeds/arabian) | Desert or Eroded Badlands |
| [Shire](breeds/shire) | Old Growth Birch Forest |
| [Haflinger](breeds/haflinger) | Stony Peaks |

Hold an [Upgraded Saddle](equipment/upgraded-saddle) while scanning so you can read breeds off the overlay without taming everything you meet.

### Pegasus, Sort Of (30-block drop)

Fit a [Horse Stabilizer](equipment/horse-stabilizer) and ride off something tall enough. Remember the stabilizer shares its slot with the [cart](equipment/horse-cart), so unhitch any cart first. A mountain edge or a deep ravine does the job.
