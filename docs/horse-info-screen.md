# The Horse Info screen

## Opening it

Press <kbd>P</kbd> **while mounted** to open a full profile of the horse you're riding.

The same key pressed while on foot [whistles](ownership-and-bonding#whistling-for-your-horse) your active horse instead. The screen only opens from the saddle.

---

## What it shows

| Field | Meaning |
|:---|:---|
| **Gender** | Male or female. Determines what it can breed with. |
| **Breed** | One of the [fifteen breeds](breeds/), or a species label for donkeys, mules, and skeleton or zombie horses. Cross-bred horses get a **(mix)** tag. |
| **Coat** | Base colour plus markings, e.g. *Chestnut + White Field*. |
| **Bond** | Current value out of 100, with a fill bar. |
| **Speed** | Blocks per second. |
| **Jump** | Block height it can clear. |
| **Health** | Hit points. |

## Reading the bars

Each stat bar fills toward the mod's ceiling, not the horse's own maximum:

| Stat | Full bar at |
|:---|:---|
| Speed | **25.5 blk/s** |
| Jump | **9.5 blocks** |
| Health | **30 HP** |

A short bar means there's room to grow. Some of that gap closes through [bonding](ownership-and-bonding#what-bond-gives-you), worth up to +75% speed and jump at 100 bond, and the rest through [breeding](genetics) for better stock.

---

## The quick stats overlay

You don't have to mount a horse to size it up. **Hold an [Upgraded Saddle](equipment/upgraded-saddle) and look at any horse**, wild or tamed, and a **Horse Stats** panel appears above your hotbar:

- Gender
- Breed
- Speed in blocks/second
- Jump height in blocks

This is the fastest way to check a wild herd before deciding which one to tame, and it works on horses you don't own.

{: .tip }
> Scanning a herd with the saddle in hand costs nothing and saves a lot of wasted taming. Look for the breed you want *and* a high starting speed, because bonding multiplies what's already there.
