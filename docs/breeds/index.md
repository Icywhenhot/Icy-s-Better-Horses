# Breeds
{: .no_toc }

Fifteen real-world breeds in five classes. The class sets your stat range and a shared set of perks; the breed decides which unique ability you get, and every one of them grows at bond 40 and bond 100.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## How breeds work

Every horse in the world now carries a **breed** alongside its gender and coat. Breed determines two things: which coat colours and markings the horse can roll, and which biomes it appears in naturally.

Breed is visible on the [Horse Info screen](../horse-info-screen), and in the quick stats overlay when you hold an [Upgraded Saddle](../equipment/upgraded-saddle) and look at a horse.

{: .note }
> **Horses already in your world keep their look.** The mod infers a breed from the coat a horse is already wearing, so an old palomino stays a palomino. Coats with no clean match are filed as [Mustang](mustang).

Donkeys, mules, skeleton horses, and zombie horses don't get a real breed. They show a species label instead.


### What each class is worth

Speed and jump are what the [Horse Info screen](../horse-info-screen) shows you, in blocks per second and blocks of jump height. A horse rolls somewhere inside its class's range at birth, and [Bond](../ownership-and-bonding) lifts it from there.

| Class | Speed (blk/s) | Jump (blk) | Health |
|:---|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| Race | 11-15 | 3-5.5 | 15-20 |
| War | 9-15 | 2-5 | 25-40 |
| Western | 8-15 | 2-4 | 15-30 |
| Pony | 8-13 | 2-4 | 25-40 |
| Draft | 7-10 | 1-3 | 35-50 |

Draft horses are the slowest and by far the toughest, and they are the only class that hauls a full wagon. Race horses are the mirror image: quickest off the mark and the first to fall over.
