# Appaloosa
{: .no_toc }

Known for its leopard-spot pattern, the only breed whose coat always carries dots. Tough and athletic.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## Class: Western

Gains speed on roads: +10% at bond 0, +20% at 40, +50% at 100.

See [Breeds](index) for what every class shares.

## Breed ability: Stock Horse

| Bond | Effect |
|:---|:---|
| 0 | night vision while ridden in the dark |
| 40 | **herding**, toggleable: livestock follow the horse with no food in hand |
| 100 | **auto-feed**: with a chest equipped, it hands nearby animals the right breeding food from its own storage |

## Coats

4 coats, each its own texture — these breeds don't use vanilla's coat and marking system.

## Where to find them

Grassland and badlands edge:

- Plains
- Wooded Badlands
- Savanna Plateau
- Sunflower Plains

{: .tip }
> Hold an [Upgraded Saddle](../equipment/upgraded-saddle) and look at a wild horse to check its breed, gender, speed, and jump before you commit to taming it.
