# American Paint
{: .no_toc }

Bold patchwork coats are the signature. Same all-purpose build as the Quarter Horse, with a dramatic white field pattern.

## Coats

{: .note }
> Always carries **White Field** markings.

| Coat |
|:---|
| Brown + White Field |
| Black + White Field |
| Chestnut + White Field |
| Dark Brown + White Field |
| White + White Field |
| Creamy + White Field |

## Where to find them

Grassland and scrub:

- Plains
- Sunflower Plains
- Savanna
- Sparse Jungle

{: .tip }
> Hold an [Upgraded Saddle](../equipment/upgraded-saddle) and look at a wild horse to check its breed, gender, speed, and jump before you commit to taming it.
