# Shire
{: .no_toc }

England's largest draft horse, taller than a Clydesdale and just as patient. Usually black or brown with white field markings.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

## Coats

{: .note }
> Always carries **White Field** markings.

| Coat |
|:---|
| Black + White Field |
| Brown + White Field |
| Gray + White Field |
| Dark Brown + White Field |

## Where to find them

Old-world forests:

- Forest
- Birch Forest
- Old Growth Birch Forest
- Windswept Forest
- Dark Forest

{: .tip }
> Hold an [Upgraded Saddle](../equipment/upgraded-saddle) and look at a wild horse to check its breed, gender, speed, and jump before you commit to taming it.
