# Clydesdale
{: .no_toc }

Scotland's draft giant, easy to spot by its white-feathered legs and bold field markings.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## Class: Draft

Slowest and toughest. Four rows of storage, a chest *and* two riders, no cart penalty, half spook chance.

See [Breeds](index) for what every class shares.

## Breed ability: Ironclad

Equipped barding grants **25% more armour** on a Clydesdale. Charge and kick damage also read armour, so this gives a modest damage bonus.

| Bond | Effect |
|:---|:---|
| 0 | equipped barding grants 25% more armour |
| 40 | that protection extends to the rider |
| 100 | **Deflect**: projectiles are stopped before they land |

## Where to find them

Rough hill country:

- Windswept Hills
- Windswept Gravelly Hills
- Windswept Forest
- Old Growth Pine Taiga

> Hold an [Upgraded Saddle](../equipment/upgraded-saddle) and look at a wild horse to check its breed, gender, speed, and jump before you commit to taming it.
