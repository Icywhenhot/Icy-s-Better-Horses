# Mustang
{: .no_toc }

A free-roaming North American horse. Tough, weatherproof, and rarely the same coat twice.
{: .fs-5 .fw-300 }

1. TOC
{:toc}

---

## Class: War

Never spooks, never rears when struck, medkits last twice as long, hits hardest.

See [Breeds](index) for what every class shares.

## Breed ability: Wild Instincts

| Bond | Effect |
|:---|:---|
| 0 | outlines nearby hostiles and snorts a warning; heals itself slowly |
| 40 | faster self-healing, and it starts slowly healing you too |
| 100 | faster rider healing |

The survivor's horse. It notices things before you do.

## Where to find them

Open frontier country:

- Plains
- Savanna
- Windswept Hills
- Badlands
- Wooded Badlands
- Sparse Jungle

{: .tip }
> Hold an [Upgraded Saddle](../equipment/upgraded-saddle) and look at a wild horse to check its breed, gender, speed, and jump before you commit to taming it.
