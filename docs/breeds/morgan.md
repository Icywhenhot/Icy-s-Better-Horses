## Class: Western

Speed bonus on roads and paths, and the widest stat spread of any class.

See [Breeds](index) for what every class shares.

## Breed ability: Easy Keeper

The Morgan bonds faster than any other horse, from the first day. Passive time near you, golden apples, the first name tag: all of it counts for more.

| Bond | Effect |
|:---|:---|
| 0 | **1.5x** bond gain |
| 40 | **2x** bond gain |
| 100 | **Adaptable**: a slice of every other tier, including a fourth storage row and a higher step |

{: .note }
> The 1.5x at bond 0 rounds **down**, so the +1 per minute from standing nearby still pays +1. Name tags (+15) and golden apples (+3) are where the early bonus shows. From bond 40 the doubling is exact and every source benefits.

It reaches its best abilities first, and it is the only horse that does a bit of everything.
